Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order? ▼
Simply send "Hi" to our WhatsApp number (+91 79898 62623). Our bot will guide you through picking a service (Task, Ride, or AnyWork) and collecting your details.
What areas do you serve? ▼
We currently serve Bhongir, Telangana (508116) and surrounding areas. We're expanding rapidly — contact us to check availability in your area.
How long does delivery take? ▼
Average delivery time is 15–20 minutes within Bhongir. This depends on distance, agent availability, and order complexity.
How do I track my order? ▼
Once an agent accepts your order, you'll receive a live tracking link on WhatsApp. Click it to watch your delivery in real-time on a map.
What if there's a problem with my order? ▼
Message us on WhatsApp immediately with your order details. We'll resolve it as quickly as possible — most issues are sorted within 30 minutes.
How do I pay? ▼
We currently accept cash on delivery. UPI and other digital payment options are coming soon.
