📦 Orders

How do I place an order on Need2Done?

Send "Hi" to our WhatsApp number +91 79810 72623. Our AI bot will respond with a menu — choose Task, Ride, or AnyWork. Follow the prompts to provide pickup location, items, or destination. Confirm the price and your order goes live instantly.

Can I cancel my order after placing it?

Yes — you can cancel before an agent accepts your order. Once an agent has accepted and is on the way, cancellation may incur a small fee. Message us on WhatsApp to initiate a cancellation.

What services does Need2Done offer?

Task Delivery — Pick up items from shops and deliver to you. Ride — Book a quick ride to your destination. AnyWork — Custom errands like document delivery, key handoff, and more.

📍 Tracking

How do I track my order in real-time?

Once an agent accepts your order, you'll receive a unique tracking link on WhatsApp. Click it to open the live map — you'll see your agent's exact GPS location, estimated arrival time, and route — updated every few seconds.

My tracking link isn't working — what do I do?

Try refreshing the page or copying the link into your browser. If the issue persists, message us on WhatsApp with your order ID and we'll send you a fresh tracking link immediately.

💳 Pricing

How is the delivery fare calculated?

Fares consist of a base fare (₹29–₹49 depending on service type) plus a per-km charge for distances beyond the included range. The full breakdown is shown to you before you confirm — no surprises.

Are there any hidden charges?

No. What you see at confirmation is exactly what you pay. There are no hidden platform fees, surge surprises, or cancellation charges if cancelled before agent pickup.

What payment methods are accepted?

We currently accept Cash on Delivery. UPI, Google Pay, and PhonePe support is coming soon. You'll be notified on WhatsApp when digital payments go live.

🔒 Safety

What is the OTP verification?

Every delivery is secured with a one-time password (OTP). When your agent arrives, they'll ask for the OTP you received on WhatsApp — this confirms you've received your order and releases payment to the agent.

Are the delivery agents verified?

Yes. All Need2Done agents are manually verified with ID proof and background checks before being onboarded. You can also view agent name and contact number once they accept your order.

Still need help?

Our support team is available 9 AM – 9 PM, 7 days a week. WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us.
