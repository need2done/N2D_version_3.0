## 1. Razorpay Live Account Onboarding Requirements
To enable live credit/debit card, UPI, and Netbanking payments on Need2Done:

### A. Business Documentation
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership Deed / MSME Certificate.
- [ ] Company PAN Card.
- [ ] Authorized Signatory PAN & Aadhaar (e-KYC verified).
- [ ] Bank Account Cancelled Cheque / Statement (matching legal entity name).
