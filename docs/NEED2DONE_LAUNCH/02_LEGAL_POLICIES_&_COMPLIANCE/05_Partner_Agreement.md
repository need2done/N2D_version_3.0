# Need2Done — Partner & Helper Agreement
**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Applicability:** All Independent Task Runners, Delivery Partners, and Service Providers ("Helpers" or "Partners")

---

## 1. Engagement & Independent Contractor Status
1. **Relationship:** This Agreement establishes a contractual business relationship between **Need2Done ("Platform")** and the **Partner ("Helper")**. The Partner acknowledges and agrees that they are an independent contractor and **NOT** an employee, agent, joint venturer, or partner of Need2Done.
2. **Flexibility:** Partners maintain sole autonomy to log online/offline, accept or decline task requests, and choose their working hours.

---

## 2. Onboarding & KYC Requirements
Prior to activation, each Partner must furnish valid, unexpired credentials:
* Government Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card).
* Valid Motor Vehicle Driving License (for delivery/ride tasks).
* Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC) and valid Insurance (for vehicle owners).
* Active Bank Account / UPI VPA in Partner's legal name for payouts.

---

## 3. Payouts, Commissions & Incentive Structure
1. **Fare Split:** The Partner earns task fees, distance charges, and delivery base fares as defined in the Partner rate card, minus the agreed Need2Done platform commission fee.
2. **Payout Frequency:** Partner earnings are tallied and settled on a weekly / bi-weekly cycle directly into the registered bank account/UPI ID, subject to minimum payout thresholds.
3. **Withholding / Penalties:** Need2Done reserves the right to withhold payouts in cases of proven fraud, customer theft, unauthorized task cancellation after receiving cash, or willful package damage.

---

## 4. Code of Conduct & Operational Standards by Vertical

### A. Home Services Professionals
* **In-Home Etiquette:** Maintain utmost dignity, polite communication, and cleanliness while inside customer residences. Do not enter unauthorized rooms.
* **Service Quality:** Complete all task checklists (cleaning, scrubbing, dishwashing, laundry) to high standard within the booked duration.
* **Safety & Verification:** Wear Need2Done identification badge where issued; verify booking details before commencing service.

### B. Medicine & Custom Work Delivery Runners
* **Prescription & Pharmacy Handling:** Procure medicines strictly from the designated licensed chemist; inspect tamper-evident seals and pharmacy receipt.
* **OTP Verification:** Partners MUST collect and verify the customer OTP upon completing pickup and delivery. Failure to verify OTP will invalidate task payment claims.
* **Customer Interaction:** Partners must maintain polite, professional behavior. Verbal abuse, harassment, asking for unrecorded off-platform payments, or sharing customer phone numbers is strictly prohibited.
* **Safety & Compliance:** Partners must adhere to traffic laws, wear helmets, and avoid operating under the influence of alcohol or narcotics.

---

## 5. Non-Exclusivity & Termination
* This Agreement is non-exclusive; Partners are free to work with other platforms.
* Need2Done may deactivate or permanently terminate a Partner account for:
  - Low customer ratings (< 3.8/5.0).
  - Unexplained cancellations or non-delivery of accepted orders.
  - Violation of the [Prohibited Tasks Policy](./08_Prohibited_Tasks_Policy.md).
  - Fraudulent GPS spoofing or fake completion logs.
