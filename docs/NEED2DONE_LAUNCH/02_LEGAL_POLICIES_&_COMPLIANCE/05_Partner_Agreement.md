# Need2Done — Partner & Helper Agreement
**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Applicability:** All Independent Task Runners, Delivery Partners, and Service Providers ("Helpers" or "Partners")

---

## 1. Engagement & Independent Contractor Status
1. **Principal-to-Principal Basis:** This Agreement governs the contractual business relationship between **Need2Done ("Platform")** and the **Partner ("Helper")**. The Partner agrees that they act solely as an independent service provider and **NOT** as an employee, worker, agent, or legal partner of Need2Done.
2. **Work Autonomy:** Partners maintain complete independence regarding when to log online, which tasks to accept or decline, and their working schedule.

---

## 2. Mandatory Partner KYC & Documentation
Prior to account activation and dispatch eligibility, every Partner must submit:
* Government Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card).
* Valid Motor Vehicle Driving License (for delivery runner & ride tasks).
* Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC) and valid Insurance (for vehicle runners).
* Background & Identity verification clearance.
* Active Bank Account / UPI VPA in the Partner's legal name for direct payout disbursements.

---

## 3. Payouts, Commissions & Service Fees
1. **Earnings:** Partners receive the task fee, hourly charge, distance rate, and customer tips as defined in the platform rate schedule, minus the applicable Need2Done platform service fee.
2. **Settlement Schedule:** Partner earnings are aggregated and transferred on a weekly / bi-weekly cycle directly into the registered bank account or UPI ID.
3. **Withholding & Deductions:** Need2Done reserves the right to withhold earnings in cases of confirmed fraud, customer cash misappropriation, unverified OTP submissions, or intentional parcel damage.

---

## 4. Professional Standards & Operational Conduct

### A. Home Services Helpers
* Treat customer homes with utmost care, respect, and hygiene.
* Complete all cleaning, scrubbing, dishwashing, and chore tasks thoroughly within the booked duration.
* Never enter unauthorized rooms or solicit off-platform cash bookings.

### B. Delivery Runners (Medicine, Food, Grocery, Custom Work)
* Verify merchant receipts and inspect tamper seals (especially for medicine and food deliveries).
* Mandatory OTP verification: Always obtain and confirm the customer OTP upon completing pickup and delivery.
* Adhere strictly to road safety regulations, speed limits, and traffic laws.

---

## 5. Termination & Deactivation
Need2Done reserves the right to deactivate or permanently terminate Partner accounts for:
1. Customer rating falling below acceptable threshold (< 3.8 / 5.0).
2. Refusal to verify OTP or unauthorized task abandonment.
3. Violation of the [Prohibited Tasks Policy](./08_Prohibited_Tasks_Policy.md).
4. Misconduct, verbal abuse, harassment, or fraudulent GPS manipulation.
