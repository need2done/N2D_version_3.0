# Need2Done — Cancellation Policy
**Last Updated:** October 2026

---

## 1. Overview
At **Need2Done ("N2D")**, we strive to provide reliable and swift hyperlocal task execution. Because our independent Delivery Partners (Helpers) commit their time and fuel immediately upon accepting an order, our cancellation policy balances flexibility for customers with fair compensation for Helpers.

---

## 2. Customer-Initiated Cancellations by Service Vertical

### A. Home Services Bookings
* **Cancellation > 2 Hours before Slot:** **₹0 (100% Free Cancellation)**. Full refund if prepaid.
* **Cancellation < 2 Hours before Slot:** **₹50 - ₹100** convenience deduction to compensate the allocated service professional.
* **Cancellation after Helper Arrival at Customer Address:** **Base Service Minimum Fare** (to cover helper commute and blocked schedule).

### B. Medicine Delivery & Custom Work Tasks
| Order Stage | Cancellation Fee | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending / Searching for Helper** | **₹0 (No Fee)** | Order can be cancelled instantly via WhatsApp Bot without any deduction. |
| **Helper Assigned (Within 2 mins)** | **₹0 (Grace Period)** | Customer may cancel without penalty if cancelled within 2 minutes of helper acceptance. |
| **Helper Assigned & En Route to Pharmacy/Store** | **₹20 - ₹50 (Travel Fee)** | To compensate the Helper for distance and fuel incurred towards pickup location. |
| **Items / Medicines Purchased at Pharmacy** | **100% of Delivery Fee + Actual Bill** | As per pharmacy regulations, medicines are non-returnable once invoiced. Full pharmacy bill is payable. |
| **Order Out for Delivery / En Route** | **Non-cancellable (Full Fare Due)** | Once items are in transit to destination, the order cannot be cancelled without full settlement. |

> [!NOTE]
> **Upcoming Services:** Cancellation rules for future service verticals (General Handyman, Groceries) will follow corresponding service category schedules.

---

## 3. Partner / Helper-Initiated Cancellations
* If an assigned Helper cancels or is unresponsive for more than 5 minutes, Need2Done's automated dispatcher will automatically reassign the next nearest available Helper at **no extra cost** to the customer.
* If no alternate Helper is available, the order is cancelled automatically and any prepaid amount is **100% refunded immediately**.

---

## 4. Platform-Initiated Cancellations
Need2Done reserves the right to cancel orders under the following circumstances:
1. Contact information or delivery address provided is incomplete or unserviceable.
2. The requested task violates our [Prohibited Tasks Policy](./08_Prohibited_Tasks_Policy.md) (e.g. hazardous materials, restricted substances).
3. The customer is unreachable for over 10 minutes upon Helper arrival at pickup or drop-off destination.
4. Extreme weather emergencies, curfews, or local law enforcement restrictions preventing safe transit.

---

## 5. How to Cancel
1. Send `Cancel Order` or select `Manage Order -> Cancel` on the Need2Done WhatsApp Bot.
2. Alternatively, reach our live support desk via WhatsApp or support portal.
