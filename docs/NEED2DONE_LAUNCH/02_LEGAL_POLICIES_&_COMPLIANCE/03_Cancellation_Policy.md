# Need2Done — Cancellation Policy
**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Effective Date:** Immediate upon order booking.

---

## 1. Overview
At **Need2Done ("N2D")**, we aim to provide maximum scheduling flexibility for customers while ensuring fair compensation for independent delivery runners and service helpers who commit their time, fuel, and equipment upon booking confirmation.

---

## 2. Cancellation Rules by Service Category

### A. Home Services
| Timing of Cancellation | Cancellation Fee | Refund / Adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| **More than 2 hours before scheduled slot** | **₹0 (Free)** | 100% full refund of any prepaid amount |
| **Within 2 hours of scheduled slot** | **₹50** | Balance refunded to original payment mode |
| **After Helper arrival at customer premises** | **Base Service Rate** | Helper visit compensation; balance refunded |

---

### B. Medicine, Food & Grocery Delivery Tasks
| Order Stage | Cancellation Fee | Liability & Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending / Searching for Helper** | **₹0 (Free)** | Immediate cancellation via WhatsApp bot |
| **Helper Assigned (Within 2 minutes)** | **₹0 (Grace Period)** | No deduction |
| **Helper En Route to Store / Pharmacy** | **₹20 - ₹40 (Travel Base)** | Covers helper transit fuel and time |
| **Items / Medicines Purchased at Merchant** | **100% of Bill + Delivery Fee** | Invoiced medicines, food, and perishables cannot be returned to merchants |
| **Out for Delivery / In Transit** | **Full Order Value** | Non-cancellable once transit has commenced |

---

### C. Custom Work & Errand Tasks
| Task Status | Cancellation Fee |
|---|---|
| **Before Helper acceptance** | **₹0 (Free)** |
| **After acceptance & travel started** | **₹20 - ₹50 (Distance dependent)** |
| **After task execution commenced** | **Pro-rata task rate + Incurred expenses** |

---

## 3. Partner or Platform-Initiated Cancellations
* **Helper Non-Availability:** If an assigned helper is delayed or cancels, our automated dispatcher reallocates the nearest alternate helper at **zero additional charge**.
* **Unserviceable Task:** If no helper is available, or if the pickup location is unserviceable, the order is cancelled automatically with a **100% immediate refund**.
* **Policy Violations:** Need2Done reserves the right to cancel any task that violates our [Prohibited Tasks Policy](./08_Prohibited_Tasks_Policy.md).

---

## 4. How to Cancel
1. Open your active WhatsApp conversation with Need2Done and type `Cancel Order` or select `Manage Booking -> Cancel`.
2. For Web Home Services bookings, click `Cancel Booking` from your live confirmation link or contact support.
