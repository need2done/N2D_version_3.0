# Need2Done — Refund Policy
**Last Updated:** October 2026

---

## 1. Overview
At **Need2Done ("N2D")**, customer satisfaction is at the core of our operations. This Refund Policy outlines how refund claims are assessed, approved, and disbursed for transactions processed through the Need2Done platform.

---

## 2. Eligibility for Refunds

You are eligible for a partial or full refund under the following verified conditions:
1. **Unfulfilled Orders:** Prepaid orders where no helper was assigned or the platform cancelled the task due to technical/operational reasons (100% refund).
2. **Missing / Undelivered Items:** Documented failure of delivery of purchased goods where payment was made in advance.
3. **Severe Transit Damage:** Damage directly caused by Helper mishandling during transit (subject to our [Customer Damage & Liability Policy](./07_Customer_Damage_and_Liability_Policy.md)).
4. **Duplicate / Erroneous Deductions:** Technical glitch resulting in multiple debits for a single transaction.

---

## 3. Non-Refundable Scenarios & Category Rules
Refunds will **NOT** be granted under the following circumstances:
* **Medicines & Pharmaceuticals:** As mandated by Indian drug retail guidelines, prescription medicines and pharmaceuticals purchased from licensed chemist shops cannot be returned or refunded once dispensed and handed over to the courier partner, except in cases of verified dispensing mismatch by the pharmacy.
* **Perishable Goods:** Food, milk, vegetables, or temperature-sensitive goods purchased per customer specification where customer was unreachable.
* **Home Services Quality Claims:** For Home Services, in the event of minor service dissatisfaction, Need2Done will dispatch a **Free Re-Service / Touch-Up visit** within 24 hours rather than a direct cash refund unless gross negligence is proven.
* **Incorrect User Information:** Incorrect address, landmark, or invalid prescription provided by the customer leading to dispatch failure.

> [!NOTE]
> **Upcoming Services:** Specialized refund terms for future verticals (Handyman, Grocery) will be published upon launch.

---

## 4. Refund Processing Timelines & Modes

| Payment Mode | Processing Time | Settlement Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **UPI / Instant Bank Transfer** | 2 to 24 Hours | 24 - 48 Hours |
| **Credit / Debit Cards (Razorpay)** | 24 Hours | 5 - 7 Business Days (Bank dependent) |
| **Need2Done In-App / Bot Credits** | Instant | Instantaneous for future orders |

---

## 5. How to Request a Refund
1. Raise a ticket via the **Need2Done WhatsApp Bot** by typing `Support` or `Refund Request`.
2. Provide your **Order ID**, **Description of Issue**, and **Photographic Proof** (if damaged/incorrect item).
3. Claims must be submitted within **24 hours** of order completion or scheduled delivery time.
4. Our Support Team will review and resolve the request within **24 business hours**.
