# Need2Done — Refund Policy
**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Effective Date:** Immediate upon transaction processing.

---

## 1. Overview
At **Need2Done ("N2D")**, customer satisfaction and transparent billing are paramount. This Refund Policy defines eligibility, assessment standards, processing modes, and banking turnaround times for refund claims.

---

## 2. Eligibility for Refunds
You are entitled to a full or partial refund under the following verified conditions:
1. **Unfulfilled / Cancelled Orders:** Prepaid orders cancelled before helper dispatch or where the platform could not allocate a service partner (100% refund).
2. **Missing / Undelivered Items:** Documented non-delivery of items purchased on your behalf.
3. **Severe Package / Item Damage:** Verified damage caused directly by courier partner mishandling during transit (subject to our [Customer Damage & Liability Policy](./07_Customer_Damage_and_Liability_Policy.md)).
4. **Duplicate / Over-Billing:** Technical glitch or duplicate gateway deduction.
5. **Home Services Quality Deficiency:** For Home Services, where service fell substantially short of stated standards, Need2Done will provide a **Free Re-Service / Touch-up Visit** within 24 hours or issue a proportional refund.

---

## 3. Non-Refundable Scenarios
Refunds will **not** be issued under the following circumstances:
* **Medicines & Pharmaceuticals:** As per statutory Drug & Cosmetic regulations, medicines purchased from licensed pharmacies cannot be returned or refunded once dispensed, except for verified merchant dispensing errors.
* **Perishable Produce & Cooked Food:** Where items were procured correctly as per customer order, but delivery failed due to customer being unreachable or providing an incorrect address.
* **Merchant Product Quality:** Taste, fit, manufacturer warranty, or original retail packaging issues of goods where the Helper correctly procured the specified brand/product.

---

## 4. Refund Processing Timelines & Modes

| Payment Method | Initiated By Us | Banking Settlement Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **UPI / Instant Bank Transfer** | Within 24 Hours | 24 - 48 Hours |
| **Credit / Debit Cards (Razorpay)** | Within 24 Hours | 5 - 7 Business Days (Bank dependent) |
| **Net Banking** | Within 24 Hours | 3 - 5 Business Days |
| **Need2Done In-App / Bot Credits** | Instant | Immediate (Usable on next booking) |

---

## 5. How to Raise a Refund Claim
1. Open the Need2Done WhatsApp Bot and type `Refund Request` or `Support`.
2. Provide your **Order ID**, **Reason for Claim**, and **Clear Photos/Receipts** (for damaged or missing goods).
3. Claims must be submitted within **24 hours** of order completion or delivery time.
4. Our Customer Support & Dispute Desk will investigate and resolve your request within **24 business hours**.
